Your privacy matters. This policy explains what Glowth does — and does not — do with your information.

Data we collect

Glowth has no sign-up and no email or password. Your goals, progress, profile and preferences are stored only on your device. The only data that reaches a server is described under “Friends & challenges” below, and it never includes your habits, progress history or Apple Health data.

On-device storage

Goal and progress data is saved in local storage on your phone. It never leaves your device unless you explicitly share or back it up yourself.

Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Glowth reads your steps, water, exercise minutes and workouts, mindful minutes and sleep so that matching goals fill themselves in. When you log progress by hand on one of those goals, Glowth also writes that entry to Apple Health, and it only ever removes entries it added itself.

Health data stays on your device: the numbers Glowth reads are kept only in the app’s local storage, and they are never uploaded to our servers, iCloud or anyone else, never used for advertising or marketing, and never sold. You decide which data types Glowth may read or write, and you can change or revoke this at any time in the Health app (your profile → Apps → Glowth) or turn the connection off in Glowth’s settings.

Friends & challenges

To make friend invites and challenge counts work, the app signs in anonymously to Google Firebase (no name, email or phone number is required). If you use Friends, your friends can see the display name and avatar you choose, whether you checked in today and your current streak — only that day’s status is kept, not a history. Joining or finishing a challenge adds one to an anonymous counter for that challenge. Invite links record anonymous events (such as an invite being opened) so we can tell whether invites work.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled as local notifications on your device. We do not use remote push servers and cannot see when or whether they fire.

Purchases

Optional Glowth Pro purchases are processed by Apple and managed by RevenueCat. We never see your payment details.

Analytics & tracking

The app does not include third-party advertising or tracking SDKs, and it does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
