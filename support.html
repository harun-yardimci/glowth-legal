We're here to help you build habits that stick. Browse the quick answers below, or reach out and we'll get back to you.

Creating a goal

Tap the + button in the tab bar, name your goal, pick a type (liquid, count, progress or timer), choose a unit and target, then save. You can edit anything later.

Logging progress

Open a goal and tap a quick-add chip, or use "Log Progress" for a custom amount. Streaks, stats and badges update automatically.

Reminders

Turn on "Reminder" while creating or editing a goal and pick a time and days. Make sure notifications are allowed for Glowth in your device settings.

Daily reset

Enable "Daily Reset" for habits you want to start fresh every day (like glasses of water). Goals without it keep accumulating toward the target.

Your data

Everything is stored locally on your device — there is no account. Deleting the app removes your data.

Managing Glowth Pro

Manage or cancel a subscription anytime in Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions. Restore a previous purchase from the paywall’s "Restore" button.
